Release Notes

Treesh 3.0 Music to Live For — now with more of everything

January 15, 2025 — 6:30 PM EST
3.0

If you've been here for a minute, you know Treesh is built around the Icons—the artists we actually listen to, not what gets pushed on us.

We just pushed 3.0 live, and it's a lot. Instead of rambling, here's what actually changed.

Player

The Player Is Now Full Screen (Finally)

No more squinting at a little mini-player. Tap any track and the player takes over the whole screen. The artwork fills the background, the vinyl spins in real time (no gimmicks—it matches the playback), and the colour scheme shifts with whatever you're playing. It feels like the song has its own space now.

Karaoke

Karaoke Mode, For Real

You can follow lyrics word‑by‑word as the track plays. Not the old‑school scrolling kind—it highlights each syllable as it hits. Perfect for learning verses, rapping along, or just showing off. You can even blow it up to full‑screen karaoke if that's your thing.

Studio NEW

Instrum: A Beat Studio Inside the App

This is the one we're most excited about. Instrum is a full step sequencer built straight into Treesh. No downloads, no external gear—just your browser.

  • 10 instruments — kick, snare, hats, 808 bass, synth leads, pads, and more
  • Layer them on the grid, adjust BPM, swing, and volume
  • Play it live, save your project, or export it as a WAV

It's a pocket studio, and it actually works.

Custom

Upload Your Own Tracks

We finally added the ability to add your own music. MP3, WAV, M4A, OGG—drop your file, add cover art, fill in the metadata, and it lives right next to the Icons' catalog. Your personal library, fully integrated.

Game

What's Next? (The Lyric Game)

Pick a song. We show you a line, you guess the next one. Type it or say it out loud—voice recognition works. Build streaks, earn points, and test how well you actually know these tracks. It's harder than it sounds.

Versus

This or That: Head‑to‑Head Listening

Two tracks, 30‑second snippets, one choice. Pick which one you're feeling more. Your winner keeps racking up wins, and three skips ends your run. It's a quick way to figure out what you actually like without overthinking it.

Share

Lyric Cards You Can Share

Found a line that hit different? Now you can turn it into a shareable image card. Pick a theme, tweak the background, adjust the text size—make it yours and send it out.

You Can Actually Make It Look Like You Want

We added a ton of customisation because not everybody wants the same setup.

  • Pick any accent colour (not just the default purple)
  • Swap the background between space, aurora, solid, or upload your own image
  • Change the app font (Google Fonts or your own uploaded file)
  • Light mode, dark mode, or surface colours for the nav and song rows

Accessibility Is Built In, Not Bolted On

High contrast, reduced motion, a dyslexia‑friendly font (Atkinson Hyperlegible), text scaling, and proper keyboard focus rings. If you need it, it's there.

Parental Controls That Actually Work

Age gate is enforced. If you're under 18, explicit tracks stay hidden until a parent or guardian unlocks them with a PIN. No workarounds.

Performance

  • Cached catalog — loads instantly offline
  • Performance mode for older devices
  • Real‑time visualiser
  • Touch‑optimised (no accidental zoom)

Privacy, No Catch

Everything stays on your device. No accounts, no servers, no tracking. Favourites, playlists, uploads, settings—all local. You're not the product.

Starlites Rewards

You earn points for listening, discovering new songs, playing games, and making beats. Daily check‑ins keep your streak going. It's small, but it's a nice nod to the people actually using the app.

What We Cleaned Up

  • Library loads faster now with real‑time sync
  • Search is smarter—typos don't break it
  • Queue management is smoother (drag, reorder, remove)
  • Playlist sharing works with one tap
  • Some lyrics now come with explanations for context

What's Next?

We're already working on collaborative playlists, live listening parties, more Instrum instruments, and deeper artist profiles with event info.

Bottom Line

Treesh is black‑owned, independent, and built for the underground—artists and listeners who want something real.

3.0 is live. Go stream.
