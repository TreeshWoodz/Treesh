How to play

  1. Read the promptEach round starts with a word.
  2. Link a word to itType anything that pairs with the prompt.
    Word+Play=Wordplay
  3. Keep the chain goingYour answer sends you to a new prompt. Each answer resets the clock. When it hits zero, the chain breaks.
  4. Build streaksFaster answers score more. Every 5 in a row raises your multiplier. Wrong links cost a second and your streak.
  5. Helps are limitedEasy gives 5, Normal 3, Hard 1, Insane none. Any help resets your streak.
