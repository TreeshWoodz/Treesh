🎮 How to Play Nects

A cosmic blend of Bingo meets Connect 4!

🎯 Game Objective

Match emojis faster than your opponent to create winning patterns. First to complete a line wins the round!

🎲 Game Setup

Both you and your AI opponent receive randomly arranged emojis. When an emoji is called, race to find and tap it on your board!
