🎮 How to Play Nects

🎯 Game Objective

Nects is a cosmic blend of Bingo and Connect 4! Match emojis faster than your opponent to create winning patterns and dominate the galaxy.

🎲 Game Setup

Both you and your AI opponent receive the same set of randomly selected emojis, but they're arranged differently on each board. A random emoji is called out, and both players must find and select it on their board.

🏆 Winning Patterns

Create a connected line of selections to win the round:

  • Horizontal: Complete a full row
  • Vertical: Complete a full column
  • Diagonal: Connect corner to corner
  • Row Mode: Select all emojis in the single row

⚡ Game Modes

Grid Sizes: Choose from 3x3, 4x4, 5x5, or 6x6 boards for varying difficulty.

Row Mode: Single row challenges (1x2, 1x3, 1x4, 1x5, 1x6)

🎚️ Difficulty Levels

  • Easy: AI responds slowly, cheats allowed
  • Normal: Balanced AI speed, cheats allowed
  • Hard: Fast AI, no cheats allowed
  • Insane: Lightning-fast AI, no cheats allowed

🔮 Power-Ups (Easy & Normal Only)

  • 🔄 Swap Emoji: Exchange two emojis on your board (1 use)
  • ↔️ Swap Boards: Trade boards with opponent (1 use)
  • 🔀 Shuffle: Randomize your entire board (1 use)
  • 🤛 Shake: Shuffle opponent's board (1 use)
  • ✴️ Free Spot: Pre-select a spot for the next call (1 use)

📊 Scoring System

  • Win a round: +100 points
  • Win streak multiplier: Points × streak count
  • Using cheats: +1 to cheat counter (tracked for stats)

👁️ Viewing Opponent's Board

Click the "View Opponent's Board" button to toggle between your board and your opponent's board. Keep an eye on their progress!

🚩 Forfeit

Want to end the match? Click the Forfeit button to return to the main menu. The match continues until someone forfeits!
