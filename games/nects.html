🎮 How to Play Nects

🎯 Game Objective

Nects is a cosmic blend of Bingo and Connect 4! Match emojis faster than your opponent to create winning patterns.

🎲 Game Setup

Both you and your AI opponent receive randomly arranged emojis on your boards. A random emoji is called out, and both players must find and select it on their board. First to tap wins that turn!

🏆 Winning Patterns

Create a connected line of selections to win:

  • Horizontal: Complete a full row
  • Vertical: Complete a full column
  • Diagonal: Connect corner to corner

⚡ Game Modes

Grid Sizes: 3×3, 4×4, 5×5, or 6×6 boards

Row Mode: Single row challenges with unique emojis per player

🎚️ Difficulty Levels

  • Easy: Slow AI, cheats available
  • Normal: Balanced AI, cheats available
  • Hard: Fast AI, no cheats
  • Insane: Lightning AI, no cheats

🔮 Power-Ups (Cheats)

  • 🔀 Shuffle: Randomize unselected squares on your board
  • ✴️ Free: Mark a spot to auto-select next round
  • 🔄 Swap: Exchange two unselected emojis
  • 🤛 Shake: Shuffle opponent's unselected squares
  • ↔️ Switch: Swap entire boards with opponent
