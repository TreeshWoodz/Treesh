🎯 Game Objective

Match emojis faster than your cosmic opponent to create winning patterns. First to complete a line wins the round!

🌌 The Cosmic Board

Both you and your AI opponent receive randomly arranged emojis. When an emoji is called, race to find and tap it on your board!

⚡ Game Modes

Classic: Race against the AI in timed rounds.

Casual: Both players select simultaneously, no timer pressure.

Row Mode: Complete entire rows against the clock!

🎚️ Difficulty Levels

Easy: Slow AI, 3 powerups per match, 10s timer

Normal: Balanced AI, 3 powerups per match, 5s timer

Hard: Fast AI, 1 powerup per match, 3s timer

Insane: Lightning AI, 1 powerup per match, 2s timer

Abysmal: Extreme AI, NO player powerups, opponent has unlimited powerups, 1.5s timer
