🎮How to Play

1Tap anywhere to hop your Frea to that spot.
2Drag & release to slingshot-fling across the arena.
3Each mode has its own goal — check the guide below, or run the Tutorial from the lobby.
4Win rounds to earn ✦ Starlites and level up your profile.

🗺Mode Guide

◎Capture— hold the orb to charge your ring to 100%.
🏁Race— first flea to grab the orb up top wins.
🔥Burning Orb— don't hold the orb when the fuse blows!
🦠Infectious— dodge infected fleas, last one safe wins.
🫣Hide & Seek— disguise as objects; seeker bumps to unmask.
🏀Hoops— fling fleas through the neon hoop. Swish = 3 pts!
🧘Zen— build, decorate and vibe. No rules, no timer.

💡FAQ

Where are my settings saved?
Everything (settings, Freas, stats, Starlites) is saved on your device automatically.

The game feels slow?
Set Performance Mode to Lite in Settings → Display & Accessibility, and switch Platforms and Background to Basic for the fastest play.

Hard to spot your Frea?
Enable SpotMe to spotlight your Frea and dim everything else.
