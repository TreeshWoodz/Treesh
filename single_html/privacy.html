Privacy Policy

Last updated October 2026

The short version: You can use Treesh as a guest with no account. Everything then stays in your browser on this device.

If you create a Treesh account, your email, profile and a backup of your settings are stored with our accounts provider so they can sync to your other devices. A few features, like your custom music, are stored only on your device and are listed in section 2. You can delete your account at any time from Settings.

1. Using Treesh as a guest

Without an account, Treesh saves the following only in your browser's local storage and IndexedDB, on your device:

  • Your profile: nickname, username, birthday, zodiac sign, bio, profile picture and banner.
  • Your library: favorites, playlists, play history, lyric notes, drafts and lyric cards.
  • Music, images, fonts and backgrounds you upload.
  • App settings, page layouts made with Magic Markup, and where you left off in a song.

We never receive a copy of this data. Clearing your browser data, or using "Clear" options inside Treesh, deletes it.

2. Treesh accounts

Accounts are optional. They let you keep your Treesh on more than one device. Accounts are run on Supabase, which stores the data below on our behalf.

What we store when you sign in

  • Sign-in details: your email address and a securely hashed password. We never see your password.
  • Your public profile: username, display name, bio and profile photo.
  • A settings backup: the Treesh data saved in your browser, such as your profile details (including your birthday), playlists, favorites, play counts, lyric drafts, Magic Markup layouts and app settings.
  • Fonts and backgrounds you added, in a private storage folder that only your account can open.

Features stored only on your device

These Treesh features keep their data in your browser on the device you use them on. They are never uploaded to your account, even when you're signed in, and they don't sync to your other devices:

  • Custom music (Music Manager and My Music): the songs you upload, their cover art, details and attached lyrics.
  • Instrum Studio recordings: vocal takes you record and audio clips you import into the timeline.
  • Rhythm game beat maps made from your songs.
  • Resume playback: where you left off in a song.
  • Weather widget: the location you share for local weather.
  • Performance mode and the performer display options.
  • Music Manager sort order.

Clearing your browser data, or using the Clear options in Settings, Storage & data, permanently removes these. Export a backup first if you want to keep a copy.

How syncing works

While you're signed in, Treesh sends your changes in the background about every 30 seconds, and when you leave the app. Fonts and backgrounds upload only when they change. Signing in on a new device downloads your backup to that device. If you're offline, changes wait on your device and sync when you're back.

Who can see it

Access rules on the database mean only your signed-in account can read or change your profile row and settings backup. Your profile photo is stored at a public web link so it loads quickly on every device. Anyone who has that exact link can view the photo, so only use a picture you're comfortable sharing.

Emails

We use your email address only to sign you in and to send account emails: confirming your address, and resetting your password when you ask. We don't send marketing emails and we don't sell or share your email.

Signing out and deleting your account

  • Sign out stops syncing. Everything already on this device stays here.
  • Delete account (Settings, Account) permanently removes your account, email, profile, photo, fonts and backgrounds, and settings backup from the cloud. It can't be undone. Data saved on your devices stays there until you clear it.

3. Your birthday

Your birthday is used on your device to show your zodiac sign, numerology, daily readings and age-appropriate content. As a guest it is not sent anywhere. If you sign in, it is included in your private settings backup so your other devices show the same readings. It is never shown on your public profile.

4. Services Treesh talks to

To work, Treesh loads some content from other services. These requests come from your browser, so those services can see your IP address and basic browser details, just like visiting any website:

  • Supabase, for sign in, sync and account emails, only when you use an account.
  • Streaming the built-in songs, cover art and artist images from their hosts.
  • Looking up lyrics and rhyme suggestions when you ask for them.
  • Your daily horoscope. Only your star sign is sent. If the service is unavailable, Treesh writes your reading offline.
  • Weather for the weather widget, only if you add it and share a location.
  • Games and pages you open inside the window viewer.
  • Fonts, styles and icons from public content networks.

Each of these services has its own privacy policy. Treesh has no ads and no tracking or analytics tools.

5. Data saved in your browser

Treesh uses local storage and IndexedDB to keep your data on your device. If you sign in, a sign-in token is also kept in local storage so you stay signed in. Treesh does not use advertising cookies.

6. Microphone

The voice assistant and recording tools ask for microphone access only when you start them. Voice commands are handled by your browser's built-in speech features. Treesh does not record or keep your voice, and voice is never uploaded to your account.

7. Backups and sharing

When you export a backup, Treesh creates a file on your device. You decide where it goes. Sharing a song, lyric card or image uses your device's share sheet.

8. Children

Treesh can be used as a guest without giving us any personal information. Creating an account needs an email address, so if you're under 13, or under the minimum age for online accounts where you live, please ask a parent or guardian first. If a parent believes their child created an account, the account can be removed from Settings, Account, or by asking us to delete it.

9. Your choices and rights

  • See and edit your profile any time in Settings.
  • Download a copy of your data with Export backup.
  • Stop syncing by signing out, or erase your cloud data by deleting your account.

Depending on where you live, you may have extra rights over your personal data, such as access, correction or deletion. The tools above cover these, and you can always contact us through treesh.app.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear on this page with a new date.
