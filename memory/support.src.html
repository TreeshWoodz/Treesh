01

Getting started

Treesh works right in your browser. No download and no account needed.

Guest or account

When you first open Treesh you can create an account, sign in, or continue as a guest. As a guest, everything is saved in this browser on this device. You can sign in later from Settings and nothing is lost.

Finding your way around

The main tabs are Library, Icons (artists & models), Studios, Game and Settings. On phones they sit at the bottom. On bigger screens they're in the sidebar, next to your playlists.

Search everythingNew

One search box finds songs, artists, lyrics, games, people and settings. Before you type, you'll see your recent searches (tap the X to remove one), what's trending on Treesh and quick links. On a computer the search box opens at the top of the screen. On phones it sits at the bottom, close to your thumb.

Press Ctrl+K (⌘K on Mac) or / to search from anywhere

Top results & filtersNew

As you type, Top shows the best match first, then groups of songs, artists, lyrics, games, users and settings. Tap See all on any group, or use the filter chips (Songs, Artists, Lyrics, Games, Users, Settings) to narrow it down. The number on each chip shows how many results it has.

  1. Press ↑ or ↓ to move through the results.
  2. Press Enter to open the highlighted one.
  3. Press Esc to close search.

Voice assistant

Tap the mic to talk to Treesh: play a song, skip, change the theme ("change theme to blue"), switch to dark or light mode and more. You can make your own voice commands in Settings. Your browser needs microphone permission.

What's New popups

The first time you open a page after an update, a short recap shows what changed. Turn them off any time in Settings → What's New popups.

02

Library & music

Browse Treesh Music, build playlists and add your own songs.

Browse & play

The Library shows picks for you, the weekly top songs, genres and your playlists. Tap any song to play it, or use Shuffle all.

Favorites & dislikes

Tap the heart to save a song to your favorites. Mark songs you don't like so they show up less. Both lists live on your profile.

Playlists

Create a playlist from the Library or from any song's menu. Rename it, reorder songs and remove tracks whenever you like. Playlist covers are built from the songs inside.

Upload your own music

Use Upload music to open your Music Studio. Drag in audio files, then edit the title, artist, cover art and lyrics on each song's card.

Your uploads never leave your device

Icons

Open Icons to see Treesh artists and models, with their songs, bios and pictures.

Treesh Blog

Read stories and updates from the Treesh team without leaving the app.

03

Now Playing

The full player, with lyrics, karaoke and sound tools.

Live lyrics

Lyrics scroll along with the song. Tap a line to jump to it. Turn on Auto-show lyric meanings to see what lines mean as they play.

Karaoke mode

Karaoke mode makes the lyrics big and readable. You can also open the full-screen karaoke stage. Karaoke always uses the Classic lyric look.

Lyric themesNew

Give your lyrics a new look: Classic, Text Messages, Karaoke Neon, Typewriter, Notebook, Retro Terminal, Comic Bubbles or Polaroid Story. Themed lyrics sit right in the player, with no box around them. Your theme is used for every song, unless you pin a different one to a single song.

  1. In Now Playing, tap the swatch button next to the lyrics (or open the lyrics ⋯ menu → Lyric theme).
  2. Pick a theme. It switches straight away.
  3. Turn on Only for this song to keep that theme just for the song that's playing.
Also in Settings → Appearance → Lyric theme

Text Messages themeNew

Lyrics show up like a group chat with the artist. Each line arrives as a text bubble when it's sung, with the artist's photo, typing dots just before the next line and a "Read" receipt. The chat scrolls by itself. Tap any bubble to jump to that line. Songs without timed lyrics show every line at once.

Singer colors

Songs with more than one artist can color each singer's lines. This is off by default. To turn it on, open the Lyric theme sheet and switch on Color lines by singer, or go to Settings → Duet colors.

Equalizer, crossfade & sleep timer

Adjust bass, mids and treble with the equalizer. Crossfade blends one song into the next. The sleep timer fades out and stops playback after the time you choose.

Vinyl record player

Turn on Vinyl record player in Settings to see a spinning record instead of the cover art.

Closing sheets

On phones and tablets, swipe or flick Now Playing and most sheets down to close them. On desktop, use the close button or press Esc.

04

Lyrics & Lyric Studio

Find, write and time lyrics, then keep them in sync with the song.

Find lyrics

In Lyric Studio, use Find lyrics to search a public lyrics library for the song you're editing. Pick a result and edit anything that's off.

Sync lines to the music

Play the song and tap to stamp each line as it's sung. The preview looks just like Now Playing, so you can check your timing before you save.

Bold & italic

Mark words with **bold** or *italic* in Lyric Studio and they'll show that way in the player.

Light or dark editor

Use the sun/moon toggle in Lyric Studio to switch between a light and dark writing space.

Lyric cardsNew

Turn your favorite lines into an image card. Under Style, pick Classic or a style that matches a lyric theme: Text Messages, Neon, Typewriter, Notebook, Terminal, Comic or Polaroid. Classic keeps the color theme, cover art background, text and sticker options. Then download or share your card.

Text Messages cards always say the messages aren't really from the artist

Back up your lyrics

Export your lyrics as a backup file and import them on another device. Find the buttons in Lyric Studio, or back up everything in Settings → Transfer.

05

Studios

Make beats, edit images and restyle Treesh itself.

Instrum Studio

Program drum patterns, set the tempo, record vocals and audio, then export your track. Beats you make also earn Starlites.

Image Studio

Edit pictures and cover art with filters, text and more. Open any cover with "Edit in Image Studio".

Backdrops Studio

Make image backdrops and wallpapers with your own text and stickers, then set one as the background behind Treesh.

Music Studio

Upload your own songs, edit their details and fix cover art in one place. Drag and drop works too.

Magic Markup

Change how any page looks. Tap the wand, then hold a section to move it, or drag the purple corner dot to resize it. With Select you can pick a single element and move, resize, hide or restyle it. Changes are saved for each page, and phone layouts are saved separately.

Not available on the Settings page

Games & Treesh Active

Lyric games, the Treesh Arcade, Starlites and Hoop.

What's Next?

Guess the next lyric line before time runs out. Your best score, streak and accuracy go to your profile's Stats tab.

This or That

Pick your favorite of two songs, head-to-head, and keep your streak going.

Treesh Arcade

Play FREA!, Vocotap, Chainz, Nects, Bronze Blitz, Ebonics, Sonoko and Don't Get Caught inside Treesh. You can minimize a game and keep browsing, or open it in a new tab.

Arcade filters & searchNew

Use the chips above the games to sort and filter: All, Recently updated, Newly added, Coming soon, Age rating (All ages, 6+ or 13+), Most played by you and A–Z. Type in the small search box to find a game by name.

Most played by you counts the games you open on this device

Don't Get CaughtNew

A horror mini-game gauntlet, rated 13+. Mr. Hush only kills what he sees, so freeze when he turns around. The Hollow Bride hunts by sound, so stay quiet. Open it from the Arcade and tap Play. Headphones recommended, and it has jump scares.

Coming soon & Notify me

New games can show up in the Arcade before they launch, with a "Soon" ribbon. Tap Notify me on the game (or in its info sheet) and Treesh will let you know the next time you open the app after it goes live.

Starlites

Earn Starlites by opening Treesh daily, keeping a streak, finding new songs, listening for 30 minutes, playing games and making beats.

Hoop & Treesh Active

Hoop from Treesh is your basketball home base: courts near you on a live map, court games like 21 and HORSE, workouts, drills and a live shot-form check. Your sessions, drills, games and best form show under Treesh Active on your profile.

Game stats on your profile

Open your profile → Stats. Arcade games shows your progress in each game, for example your Vocotap level, best score and max combo. Treesh Active shows your Hoop stats. Visitors see an Arcade tab on your page, and you choose who can see it in Privacy.

Profiles & friends

Your page, your style. Visitors see it exactly how you designed it.

Your full-page profile

On big screens your profile fills the whole window: banner on top, your info card on one side and your sections, song and tabs on the other. Visitors see your page exactly the way you styled it. On phones everything stacks neatly.

Edit your profile

Open your profile and tap Edit profile to change your nickname, @username, bio, birthday, photo and banner.

Style your space

Tap Style to pick a starter theme, background (color, gradient, image or pattern), card style, blur and corners, fonts and colors.

  1. Open your profile.
  2. Tap Style.
  3. Change anything, then tap Save my space.

Profile song & mood

Choose a Treesh song that plays when people visit your page. They get play and mute buttons. Add a mood and a short status too. Songs you uploaded yourself can't be used, because they stay on your device.

Sections & Top 8

Write an About me, add "Who I'd like to meet", add up to six text blocks and pick your Top 8 friends. Reorder or hide any section, and choose whether your info card sits on the left, the right or in the center.

Talents, favorites & badges

Pick one main talent and up to five more, then list your favorite music, movies, games and food. Badges unlock as you use Treesh.

Birthdays & your stars

Add your birthday to get a birthday surprise on the day and a cake badge on your profile. Tap your zodiac sign to see your horoscope, numerology, tarot and fortune.

Friends

Search Users to find people, open their page and tap Add friend. Accept requests from Friends on your profile. You can remove friends or block someone from their page's menu.

Profile privacy

Make your profile public or private (friends only). For each page, including Favorites, Lyrics, Playlists, Badges, Friend list and Arcade & Treesh Active, choose Everyone, Friends or Only me.

Accounts & sync

An optional Treesh account backs up your profile and settings.

Create an account

Choose Create account on the welcome screen, or go to Settings → Account. Confirm your email with the link we send you, then come back to Treesh.

What syncs

Your profile, settings, playlists and lyric work sync to your account, at most once every 30 seconds. Fonts and backdrops only sync when they change. Custom music never uploads.

Confirmation link expired?

Open the link anyway. If it has expired, the page lets you send a new one. Check your spam folder too.

Delete your account

Go to Settings → Account → Delete account and type DELETE to confirm. This removes your account and the files stored with it. Data saved in this browser stays until you clear it.

Community & moderation

Treesh moderators can mute or suspend accounts that break the rules. If you're muted you can keep editing, but changes stay private until the mute ends. To report someone, block them and contact support below.

Settings & personalization

Make Treesh look, sound and feel the way you like.

Appearance & colors

Switch between light and dark mode and choose your theme, accent color, fonts, surfaces and font color.

Motion & effects

Turn the animated starfield background and interface glow on or off. Performance mode cuts animations for smoother playback.

Show alerts in the top bar instead of on screen. The explicit content switch unlocks explicit tracks, and it locks again each time you reopen Treesh.

Privacy & your data

You decide what's shared and what stays on your device.

Saved on your device

Guests keep everything in this browser: profile, library, lyrics, layouts, uploads and game progress. Clearing your browser data removes it, so back it up first.

Export & import

Settings → Transfer exports all your Treesh data, including game saves, to one backup file. Import it on another device or browser.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to the questions we get the most.

Do I need an account to use Treesh?

No. Guests can use every studio and game. An account only adds backup, sync and a public profile that friends can visit.

Is the music I upload sent to Treesh?

No. Custom music always stays on your device, even when you're signed in. That's also why you can't use your own uploads as a profile song.

Why can't visitors hear my profile song?

Some browsers block autoplay until you tap the page. Visitors can press play on your song card. Make sure the song is a Treesh song and the Profile song section isn't hidden.

My changes aren't showing on another device.

Sign in with the same account on both devices and wait up to 30 seconds. Custom music, and anything saved while you were a guest on another browser, won't move automatically. Use Settings → Transfer for that.

My Hoop or Vocotap stats aren't on my profile.

Play on treesh.app in the same browser you use for Treesh, then reopen your profile's Stats tab. Stats are read from the games on this device. Visitors see the latest numbers after your next sync.

How do I make my profile private?

Open your profile and tap Public (or Settings → Account → Privacy), then choose Private. Friends still see your page. You can also hide single pages, such as Arcade & Treesh Active.

The voice assistant doesn't hear me.

Allow microphone access when your browser asks, and use a browser that supports speech recognition (most Chromium browsers and Safari do).

How do I undo a Magic Markup change?

Open Magic Markup on that page and reset the section or element you changed. Every page keeps its own layout, so other pages aren't affected.

How do I change how lyrics look?

In Now Playing, tap the swatch button next to the lyrics and pick a theme. Turn on Only for this song to keep it for just that song. You can also change it in Settings → Appearance → Lyric theme.

Are the Text Messages lyrics real texts from the artist?

No. The Text Messages theme just shows song lyrics styled like a chat. Lyric cards made with this style always include a note saying the messages aren't from the artist.

Why are some lyric lines in different colors?

Singer colors are on, so each artist on the song has their own color. Turn it off in the Lyric theme sheet (Color lines by singer) or in Settings → Duet colors. It's off by default.

How do I report someone?

Block them from the menu on their profile so they can't contact you. Then send us their @username using the form below, and choose "Report a person".
